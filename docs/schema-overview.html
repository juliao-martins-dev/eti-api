ETI-Dili Attendance API — Schema Overview

Every model in the project, what it stores, and how the tables hang together. The system replaces the paper book “LISTA PREZENSA BA PROFESÓR/A ETI DILI”: a monthly sheet per teacher, four punches a day, a handwritten signature next to each. In the database the signature has become a photo plus GPS coordinates.

accounts

User accounts_user AUTH_USER_MODEL

Every person who can log in — teachers, administrators, and eventually students — built on AbstractUser with email as the login field (username is removed entirely). The teaching fields mirror the “Dadus Professores” table published on the school site, so a user row is both an account and the teacher’s personnel record.

attendance

ListaPrezensa attendance_listaprezensa

One printed sheet: a single teacher for a single month. It holds the header block of the paper form (Naran, Kargu, Fulan/Tinan) while the day-by-day grid lives in Prezensa. Created automatically by the first punch of a month — there is no separate “open the sheet” step.

profesor_idFK → accounts.UserFK see below.
karguCharField(120)Snapshot of the teacher’s position at the time the sheet was issued. The only field copied from the user; filled in save(), never by callers.
fulanPositiveSmallIntegerField, choicesMonth 1–12, labelled in Tetun (Janeiru…Dezembru).
tinanPositiveSmallIntegerFieldYear, validated 2000–2100.
kriadu_ihaDateTimeFieldauto_now_add — when the sheet was opened.
atualiza_ihaDateTimeFieldauto_now
Relationships
FK profesor → accounts.User · related_name lista_prezensa · on_delete CASCADE
One teacher has many monthly sheets; each sheet belongs to exactly one teacher. Deleting the user deletes their whole attendance history.
reverse FK ← Prezensa · lista.prezensa
One sheet has many day rows — up to one per working day of that month.
Constraints & behaviour
  • Unique (profesor, fulan, tinan) — a teacher cannot have two sheets for the same month.
  • Why kargu is duplicated: a promotion must not rewrite the title printed on last month’s signed sheet. Every other teacher attribute is read through profesor and never copied.
  • Ordering: newest month first, then teacher name.

Prezensa attendance_prezensa

One row of the grid — one teacher, one day. It deliberately stores no times: the four columns of the paper book are rebuilt from the Marka punches, because a punch now carries evidence that must stay attached to it.

reverse FK ← Marka · prezensa.marka
One day has up to four punches — morning in/out and afternoon in/out.
Constraints & behaviour
  • Unique (lista, data) — one row per teacher per day.
  • Prezensa.objects.ba_loron(profesor, data) is the only way rows are created: it opens the monthly sheet and the day row together.
  • Business rules live here, in clock_in() / clock_out(): no duplicate punch per session, no clock-out before clock-in, no Saturday afternoon, and the teacher must be inside the school’s 100 m radius.
  • Session cut-off is 13:00 — a punch before it belongs to DADER, at or after to LOROKRAIK.

Marka attendance_marka

One punch: the moment a teacher pressed Clock In or Clock Out, with the evidence captured at that moment. This table is the replacement for the handwritten signature — the time comes from the server and the coordinates from the device, so nothing here is the teacher’s to edit.

One day has many punches; each punch belongs to exactly one day. The chain up to a person is Marka → Prezensa → ListaPrezensa → User.
Constraints & behaviour

  • save() recomputes distansia_metru and iha_eskola from the coordinates every time.
  • Distance uses a haversine formula in attendance/geo.py — no Maps API, no PostGIS.

third-party tables

OutstandingToken & BlacklistedToken rest_framework_simplejwt.token_blacklist

Not written by this project, but part of its schema. Refresh-token rotation is on, so every refresh records the issued token and blacklists the one it replaced — this is what makes logout able to revoke a session.

FK OutstandingToken.user → accounts.User · on_delete CASCADE
One user has many issued refresh tokens — one per login and per rotation.
1:1 BlacklistedToken.token → OutstandingToken
An issued token is revoked at most once. Presence of the row means the refresh token no longer works.
  • Grows by roughly one row per refresh (~200/day for 57 teachers). Run manage.py flushexpiredtokens weekly.
  • Django’s own tables (auth_group, auth_permission, django_admin_log, django_session, django_migrations) are present and unmodified.
